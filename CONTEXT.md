# App Store Review Analysis

Analysis turns a storefront's accessible reviews into independent rating and sentiment measurements and evidence-backed improvements.

## Language

**Collection**: Unique valid reviews retained in encounter order from the requested storefront, together with counts, source attempts, and any shortfall.

**Sentiment**: The textual feedback label of positive, neutral, or negative. It is independent of the review's star rating.

**Issue**: An actionable complaint supported by one or more negative reviews. Positive and neutral reviews provide context but cannot be issue evidence.

**Evidence**: Original negative reviews cited by their exact review IDs to support an issue. Counts use unique IDs; one review can support several issues.

**Insight generation**: The process of interpreting grouped reviews, validating supported issues, and producing short prioritized improvement actions.

**Report**: The complete analysis result containing collection details, independent metrics, supported issues, actions, original reviews, and processing provenance.
